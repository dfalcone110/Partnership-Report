Partnership for Safe Water Distribution Program -- Disinfectant Residual
Update

The Bureau of Laboratory Services is responsible for administering
compliance with the water quality monitoring requirements under both the
federal Safe Drinking Water Act (SDWA) and state regulations. As
monitoring locations that were temporarily not visited after the onset
of COVID-19 are now being revisited, over 600 drinking water samples are
collected from 81 monitoring locations.

Regarding disinfectant residual, the Partnership for Safe Water has a
numerical goal that 95\% of monthly routine samples be within the
following range:

\textbf{Total Chlorine Residual \(\geq\) 0.50 mg/L and \(\leq\) 4.0
mg/L}

PWD has met this goal for Partnership Years encompassing June 1, 2017
through May 31, 2021 (the Partnership year begins June 1st and runs
through May 31st). From November 2017 -- July 2019, less than 1\% of
routine samples were below the Partnership goal of 0.50 mg/L for every
month. The last month that PWD did not meet the 95\% Partnership goal
was September 2016 (7.27\% of monthly samples were below 0.50 mg/L),
with the following two Septembers having less than 2\% of samples below
0.50 mg/L and meeting Partnership's monthly goal. The highest percentage
of monthly Partnership samples not meeting the goal in the previous
three calendar years (2023 -- 2026) was 6.8\% in Sep 2025.

A total of 691 samples were collected in May 2026, down from 732 samples
collected in April. 488 field chlorine residual samples were collected
in accordance with DRR/RTCR requirements. PWD adjusted the sampling
schedule and frequency starting in the second half of March 2020 due to
the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic. PWD is still collecting more than the
required 360 samples for RTCR/DRR compliance. Starting in November 2020,
PWD began to resume sampling from some routine fire and police stations
for the RTCR/DRR compliance program. The selected fire and police
stations are the locations that are sampled monthly as part of the DBP
monitoring program as well. PWD also began resuming sample collection
samples from additional fire and police stations earlier this year that
were not being sampled during the first year of the pandemic. Starting
in July 2020, PWD resumed monthly distribution Disinfection By-Product
sampling from the normal fire and police station locations. As of
October 2021, Chestnut Hill Hospital is no longer be sampled from and
the Chestnut Hill Pressure District Pump Station replaced the hospital
as the routine sampling location for the Chestnut Hill pressure
district.

\textbf{May 2026 had 0 samples below 0.50 mg/l throughout the
distribution system, and 2 samples below 1 mg/l.}
