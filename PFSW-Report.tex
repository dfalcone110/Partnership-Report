Partnership for Safe Water Distribution Program -- Disinfectant Residual
Update

The Bureau of Laboratory Services is responsible for administering
compliance with the water quality monitoring requirements under both the
federal Safe Drinking Water Act (SDWA) and state regulations. As
monitoring locations that were temporarily not visited after the onset
of COVID-19 are now being revisited, over 600 drinking water samples are
collected from 63 monitoring locations.

Regarding disinfectant residual, the Partnership for Safe Water has a
numerical goal that 95\% of monthly routine samples be within the
following range:

\textbf{Total Chlorine Residual ≥ 0.50 mg/L and ≤ 4.0 mg/L}

PWD has met this goal for Partnership Years encompassing June 1, 2017
through May 31, 2021 (the Partnership year begins June 1st and runs
through May 31st). From November 2017 -- July 2019, less than 1\% of
routine samples were below the Partnership goal of 0.50 mg/L for every
month. The last month that PWD did not meet the 95\% Partnership goal
was September 2016 (7.27\% of monthly samples were below 0.50 mg/L),
with the following two Septembers having less than 2\% of samples below
0.50 mg/L and meeting Partnership's monthly goal. The highest percentage
of monthly Partnership samples not meeting the goal in the previous
three calendar years (2019 -- 2021) was 2.78\% in September 2020.

A total of 766 samples were collected in August 2023, up from 646
samples collected in July. 525 field chlorine residual samples were
collected in accordance with DRR requirements. 523 field chlorine
residual samples were collected in accordance with RTCR requirements.
PWD adjusted the sampling schedule and frequency starting in the second
half of March 2020 due to the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic. PWD is still
collecting more than the required 360 samples for RTCR/DRR compliance.
Starting in November 2020, PWD began to resume sampling from some
routine fire and police stations for the RTCR/DRR compliance program.
The selected fire and police stations are the locations that are sampled
monthly as part of the DBP monitoring program as well. PWD also began
resuming sample collection samples from additional fire and police
stations earlier this year that were not being sampled during the first
year of the pandemic. Starting in July 2020, PWD resumed monthly
distribution Disinfection By-Product sampling from the normal fire and
police station locations. As of October 2021, Chestnut Hill Hospital is
no longer be sampled from and the Chestnut Hill Pressure District Pump
Station replaced the hospital as the routine sampling location for the
Chestnut Hill pressure district.

\emph{August 2023 had 7 samples below 0.50 mg/l throughout the
distribution system, and 28 samples below 1 mg/l.}

Total THM values decreased compared to last month. 2 locations had
single sample TTHM results above the MCL of 80 ppb. Single sample TTHM
results above the MCL are not an MCL exceedance as compliance with the
MCL is based on Locational Running Averages.

Total HAA5 values increased compared to last month. 3 locations had
single sample HAA5 results above the MCL of 60 ppb. Single sample HAA5
results above the MCL are not an MCL exceedance as compliance with the
MCL is based on Locational Running Averages.
